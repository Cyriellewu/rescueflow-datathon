    Is the simulation realistic? Our assumptions checked against real ambulance records

    Every simulated ambulance goes to the scene, drives the patient to hospital and hands them over. We compared how long each step takes in our simulation with real records from New York City (FDNY), San Francisco, Indiana and Riverside County, CA. Other cities are only a rough reference, not proof about New York. The table gives one verdict per step; the full details are below.
