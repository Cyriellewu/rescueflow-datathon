## Numbers you may be asked about (all in docs/METHODS.md)

- Data: FDNY EMS Incident Dispatch Data (NYC Open Data 76xm-jjuj), 2005-01 → 2026-09, ~30 M rows fetched; model uses 2023-01 → 2026-09 (6.0 M cleaned rows, 164 k borough-hours). Extra sources: NYC DOT traffic speeds, Open-Meteo weather, NYS DOH hospitals, NYC DCP boundaries, DOHMH ZIP areas.
- Splits: train 2023-01-29 → 2025-06-30, validation 2025-07 → 2025-12, test 2026-01 → 2026-09. No tuning on test.
- Model: XGBoost, 45 features, 600 trees, depth 3. Test PR-AUC 0.595 (0.563–0.621); demand-only 0.471; persistence 0.364. Precision 0.69, recall 0.31, 4.0 false alerts/day at the 10% budget; High alert tier (5%) precision 0.78; onset warning rule lifts onset recall 0.094 → 0.202 at the same alert rate as a raised line (0.139).
- Nulls: LightGBM, CatBoost, ensemble within 0.002; 2005+ and 2016+ training no gain; traffic/weather/call-type no gain; sub-hour features +0.002; onset specialist equal to the primary.
- Lab (simulation, 20 seeds): AI-timed reserves vs none −0.91 min low-acuity mean wait (−1.31 to −0.51); vs fixed schedule −0.58 (−0.97 to −0.19); arrivals oracle +0.39 worse than the model; relocation never helps. Holds under a realistic 41-min hospital time with smaller effect.
- LinuxONE: s390x, 3 vCPU, Ubuntu 24.04; model there is sklearn HistGradientBoosting (no xgboost wheel), PR-AUC 0.596; scoring p50 2.3 ms; replay 257 k events/s one core, 577 k three cores (2.24×), 2.2 M× real time; 140 lab runs in 124 s; lab replication: AI vs none −0.46 (apart), AI vs fixed −0.15 (not separable).
