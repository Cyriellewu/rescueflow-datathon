Generated by `scripts/performance_audit.py`. 1,572,634 calls, 2025-01-06 → 2026-01-04; severity 1–3 = 440,709. This measures FDNY's historical operational performance from public records; it is not an accuracy score for FDNY's CAD, whose internal predictions and unit states are not public. Volume alone leaves most of the variation unexplained, which is what the queue state is meant to capture.

## Decision

Recommended label target: **`dispatch`**, by hour-to-hour variability (rise too small to attribute) (hour-to-hour CV: dispatch 0.53, travel 0.15). `python scripts/run_pipeline.py --target dispatch ...`. Rule: predict the stage that accounts for most of the rise in mean high-acuity response; check it against sections 3–5 before committing, and record the choice in PLAN.md.
